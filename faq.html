Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about the ILM Test Center prototype and its planned capabilities.

What is ILM Test Center?

It is an online assessment workspace concept for creating tests, managing participants and reviewing results.

Can students take live online tests right now?

The current GitHub Pages version is a front-end prototype. Secure live test delivery and backend services are not connected yet.

Are results saved permanently?

No. Current demo data is stored temporarily in the browser session or page code. A secure database must be connected for real student records.

Does AI generate questions?

An AI question assistant is a planned capability. It is not connected to an AI service in this prototype.

Does the platform monitor cameras or screens?

No monitoring is active in this prototype. Any future supervision feature must be transparent, optional where appropriate, and configured with clear notice and consent.

Are Google Forms, Moodle or Classroom connected?

No. Their pages currently describe integration concepts only. They require development and authorized account connections.

How can our school contact ILM?

Use ilmthecontestcenter@gmail.com to discuss school assessments, competitions or collaboration.
